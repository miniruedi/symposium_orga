\begin{conf-abstract}
{Vermifiltration gas emissions and mass balances}
{Kayla Coppens}
{Eawag / University of Geneva}
{The miniRUEDI was used with a static flux chamber to quantify \ce{CO2} and \ce{CH4} emissions from 6 laboratory-scale vermifilters treating blackwater and brownwater at varying hydraulic loading rates. The gas emissions were further used to explore the impact of wastewater type and hydraulic loading on nitrogen and carbon mass balances.}
\end{conf-abstract}
