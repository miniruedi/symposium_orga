\begin{conf-abstract}
{Impact river work entangled with gas data}
{Théo Blanc, Friederike Currle, Morgan Peel ...etc... Brunner Roki Schilling}
{Universiy of Neuchatel / Eawag}
{Entangle the travel time and mixing ratios from freshly infiltrating river water into groundwater. Use environmental gas tracers miniRuedi and Rad 7. Binary mixing models coupled with excess air model. The travel times change drastically because of riverwork and high discharge event.}
\end{conf-abstract}
