{Membrane Inlet Mass Spectrometry (MiniRuedi) is a powerful tool for measuring dissolved gases in real time directly in the field. Recent studies have shown that changes in gas composition detected with this technique can be linked to activity in hydrothermal and tectonic systems.  While measuring \ce{^3He}/\ce{^4He} ratios could also provide valuable information, the MiniRuedi cannot measure these directly, and collecting suitable samples is not easy. However, since the MiniRuedi can track several dissolved gases at once--and because changes in these gases have been connected to seismic activity--it can be used to trigger the collection of gas samples. These samples can then be sent to a noble gas laboratory for precise measurement of \ce{^3He}/\ce{^4He} ratios.  Here, we introduce a prototype sampling box designed to collect and preserve gas samples for high-precision analysis.}
