\begin{conf-abstract}
{Evaluating the effects of multiple variable on the dissolution of \ce{CO2}}
{Mohammed Alshareef, Darren Hillegonds, Chris Ballentine}
{University of Oxford}
{In a controlled tube experiment, we simulated the injection of \ce{CO2} through a vertical water column to investigate how bubble size and injection flux influence \ce{CO2} dissolution dynamics. Continuous gas composition data were collected from the headspace using a miniRUEDI, allowing real-time monitoring of \ce{CO2} concentrations. Analysis of the time-series data reveals distinct signatures associated with each variable, providing insights into the relationship between bubble dynamics, gas dissolution efficiency, and flux behavior.}
\end{conf-abstract}
