Dear Colleagues and miniRUEDI Friends

Please feel invited to the miniRUEDI Symposium 2025!

    What: Science and techniques of novel (on-site) gas analysis in terrestrial and aquatic systems. Presentations may focus on scientific or technical aspects and everything in between. The symposium will be held informal, and we welcome presentations of ongoing work that is still “under construction”.
    When: 18-20 November 2025
    Where: Eawag, Dübendorf/Zurich, Switzerland

Further information and the registration form will be published at www.miniruedi.net/symposia/2025 as soon as these details become available. We will also send a follow-up message when the time comes.

Please don't hesitate to share this information with your colleagues.

We are looking forward to seeing you in Dübendorf / Zurich!
